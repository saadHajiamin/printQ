How we estimate your wait
  • Printing speed: black & white 3 sec per page, colour 7.5 sec per page, A3 is 1.6x slower
  • Each job has setup time (20 sec, more for colour and A3) plus 3 sec per copy to collate
  • Finishing is per copy: staple 8 sec, spiral bind about 70 sec plus a little per page
  • Switching the printer between colour and B&W adds 25 sec, between A4 and A3 adds 20 sec
  • The job being printed counts down from when it started. Your wait is everything ahead of you plus your own job
